### Phrasing to avoid

- **"The only private forensic behavioral profiler in California."** Not substantiated, and
  there are 1,000+ licensed PIs in California to be wrong about. **Don't reach for a softer
  superlative either** ("one of the few", "among the very few") — those are still countable
  claims and they read as hedging.

  **Convey rarity structurally instead: state the two facts and let the reader draw the
  conclusion.** This is both safer and more persuasive, because every piece of it is checkable:

  > The combination is uncommon by its nature. Behavioral analysts rarely hold a private
  > investigator's license. Private investigators rarely hold a graduate degree in forensic
  > psychology. Greg A. Tucker holds California PI license #188351 and a Master of Science in
  > Forensic Psychology, and works both sides of the same case.

  Use that construction anywhere the rarity needs making. If Greg later produces something
  specific and verifiable that no other California PI has — a certification register, a
  membership roster — a narrow factual claim can replace it.
- **Anything implying clinical licensure.** Greg's forensic psychology degree is academic. He
  is not a licensed clinical psychologist and does not perform psychological evaluations,
  diagnoses, or custody recommendations to the court.
- **Promises of case outcomes** ("win your custody case"). California courts decide custody on
  the best interest of the child.

### Julia Tucker — title problem

The site calls Julia a "psychologist." She does not hold a California psychologist licence:

- A Board of Psychology search for **JULIA TUCKER returned 0 results.**
- Across **all** DCA boards, 57 "Julia Tucker" records exist. The Board of Psychology is not
  among them, and no `Psychologist` or `Licensed Educational Psychologist` licence type appears.
- Independently of the search: **California psychologist licensure requires a doctorate.**
  Julia's stated credentials are master's degrees in Education and Child Psychology, which
  cannot support that licence.

California Business & Professions Code §2903 restricts the title "psychologist." **This needs
correcting on `/about` regardless of what Greg decides about anything else on this list.**

Accurate wording that keeps the differentiator and loses the exposure — already used on
`/high-net-worth-child-custody`:

> Julia Tucker, Co-Owner and COO, brings a background in child psychology and education, a
> paralegal certification, and more than 17 years working with children and families.

_Search limits worth stating: this covered the name "Julia Tucker." A licence held under a
different legal or maiden name, or in another state, would not appear._

## Why this file exists

A search for Greg on 2026-10-01 returned nine results; four were gatuckerpi.com itself. When an
AI assistant is asked about the firm, it is largely reading his own marketing and paraphrasing
it back. That produces a flattering answer today and a hedged one the moment a more cautious
model weighs single-sourced claims.

Independent sources that already exist: BBB, Yelp, Yahoo Local, Facebook, the California LLC
registry. Sources that should exist and do not yet: a CALI "Find An Investigator" listing, a WAD
listing, attorney bios naming him as their investigator, and a Google Business Profile that
actually surfaces in search.

Every one of those is free. Each one that repeats the facts above makes the next AI answer
harder to hedge.
