# Blog hero prompts — batch 2 (19 images)

_Created 2026-10-19. Covers every post still running a recycled service-page hero._

After this batch **all 26 posts have their own image.** Batch 1 delivered 7 (hidden assets,
guardianship, profiling myths, victimology, forensic linguistics, expert witness, executive
due diligence). These 19 cover the rest.

## How to use

Generate all 19, drop them in a folder, tell me where. I'll crop any borders, normalise to
**1365x768**, convert to WebP, write alt text from the actual rendered file, and wire each to
its post. If one comes out badly, say so and I'll keep the existing image on that post.

**Settings:** generate at the nearest native 16:9 and let it fill the frame — do not let the
model pad to reach the ratio, that bakes in letterbox bars. Two of the first eight came back
with them. Expect 3-5 attempts on anything with fine mechanical detail.

## Rules baked into every prompt below

No people, no faces, no hands. No children (items only). No readable text, labels, names,
dates, or numbers anywhere in frame — **this is the one that failed last time**, a logbook
came back with garbled nonsense lettering on the cover. No badges, seals, or law-enforcement
signifiers. No peering into windows or hidden-camera POV. No trench coats, fedoras,
magnifying glasses, fingerprints, or hacker aesthetics.

## Style, shared by all 19

Near-black environment (#0D0D0D–#1A1A1A), a single warm practical light source (#CEA53D)
against cool blue-grey shadow, shallow depth of field, 35mm film-still grain. Subject sits
**right-of-centre**; the **left two-thirds stays dark and low-detail** so the site can overlay
a large white headline. Full-bleed 16:9, no borders.
