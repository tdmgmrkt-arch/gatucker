# Private Investigator Service Area — Southern California and Beyond

GA Tucker PI is a licensed California private investigator (CA PI #188351) based in Murrieta, CA. Greg Tucker serves clients throughout Southern California, the greater Los Angeles metro, the Inland Empire, and select locations statewide — including San Francisco — for cases that require in-person investigation or remote records work.

When geography matters, GA Tucker PI travels. When it does not — for background checks, skip traces, records research, and litigation support — the firm operates efficiently regardless of where the subject or attorney is located.

## How Service Area Affects Your Case

**For surveillance:** Proximity matters. The further from Murrieta, the more a case may involve travel time and coordinated logistics. Greg will discuss this during your initial consultation so there are no surprises.

**For background checks and skip traces:** Geography is largely irrelevant. Subjects anywhere in the United States can be researched using licensed investigative databases. Turnaround is typically 1-2 business days.

**For court-related work:** GA Tucker PI is familiar with courthouse procedures in Riverside County Superior Court (Murrieta, Riverside, and Indio branches), Los Angeles Superior Court, and San Bernardino County Superior Court. If your case requires physical court records retrieval, that familiarity matters.
